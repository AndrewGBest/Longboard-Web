Websites are built for a mouse or a finger.

Longboard makes them usable with six buttons. It has three ways to drive a page, and there is always a next one to try.

  1. First

    A profile, when the site has one

    A small settings file for one website that tells Longboard how it is laid out: its rows, its grids, its player. A reviewed profile is used by itself.

  2. No profile?

    Generic mode, for everything else

    Longboard finds the things you can press and moves the highlight between them by where they sit on screen. Any website works to some degree.

  3. Still stuck?

    A pointer, when you need one

    One button gives you an on-screen cursor, moved with the arrows or a controller's stick, for maps, canvases and pages the highlight cannot handle.

Profiles are data, not programs, so anyone can make one. Write it, export it as a file, import someone else's, or submit it to the shared catalogue.

What's in it

A whole browser, arranged so that every part of it can be reached from a remote.

Any website can be an app

Add a site to Home and to your TV's app list, and it opens like an app.

Type from a phone, or speak

Scan a code and a phone on your home network becomes the keyboard. Speech is worked out on your machine, in English for now.

Video keys that work

With a video full screen, the buttons become play, pause and seek. A page that is not in front is stopped.

An ad-blocking switch per site

It refuses requests a public filter list names. The switch is yours: Longboard never flips it for you.

Private by default

No telemetry, analytics or crash upload. Sites are asked about before they get the camera, microphone or your location.

The rest of a browser

Tabs and private tabs, history, reader view, a parental lock with a PIN, saved passwords, and extensions from the Chrome Web Store, though not all of them work.

Releases are not signed. The ARM packages are built and tested on ARM machines and have not yet been tried on a TV box. There is no Windows, macOS or Android TV version.

Before you install

Longboard is an early build. Expect rough edges, and know what you are getting.

What is missing

  • No Safe Browsing. Nothing warns about known phishing or malware sites.
  • No sync between machines and no translation. Tabs are not kept when Longboard is closed.
  • Passwords are kept only on a system with a keyring, and filled in only when asked. There is no other autofill.
  • Ad blocking only refuses requests. It does not hide the gaps ads leave, and it does nothing about advertising inside a video stream.
  • USB, serial and screen capture are refused to sites.

What it will not do

  • It does not remove or work around DRM.
  • It never claims to be a TV or another device to get better video quality.
  • It does not skip ads inside video or download streaming video.
  • It sends no telemetry.
