Websites weren't made for a remote. Longboard fixes that.

Longboard works out what you can press on a page and lets you move between it all with the arrows. It has three ways of doing this, so you are never stuck.

  1. The best experience

    Sites with a profile feel like TV apps

    A profile teaches Longboard the layout of one website: its rows, its menus, its video player. When a checked profile exists for the site you open, Longboard uses it automatically.

  2. Everywhere else

    Longboard finds its own way around

    On any other website, Longboard spots the links and buttons by itself and moves the highlight between them. It works on every site, though some are smoother than others.

  3. For tricky pages

    A pointer when you need one

    Some pages, like maps, are made for a mouse. One button gives you an on-screen pointer that you steer with the arrows or a controller's stick. Press it again to go back.

Anyone can make a profile. Profiles are simple settings files, not code. Write one for your favourite site, share it with a friend, or add it to the shared collection so it works for everyone.

Made for the living room

Everything you expect from a browser, and all of it within reach of your remote.

Turn any website into an app

Pin a site to your home screen and to your TV's app list. It opens just like any other app.

Type with your phone or your voice

Scan a code on the TV and type from your phone. Or just speak: your voice is turned into text on your own device and never leaves it. English only for now.

Your remote controls the video

When a video is full screen, your buttons play, pause and skip. Leave the page and the video stops, so nothing keeps playing in the background.

Ad blocking you control

Longboard can block ads on web pages, with an on/off switch for every site. Your choices stay exactly as you set them.

Private by design

No tracking and no data collection. A website has to ask you before it can use your camera, your microphone or your location.

All the usual browser features

Tabs, private tabs, history, a reader view, saved passwords, a parental lock with a PIN, and extensions from the Chrome Web Store (not every one works).

Six buttons do everything

Up, down, left, right, OK and Back. Any other button is a shortcut, never a requirement.

Get Longboard

Longboard runs on a Linux computer connected to your TV, such as a mini PC or a Raspberry Pi. You always get the latest version here.

Your TV computer

AppImage Easiest

A single file with nothing to install, and it keeps itself up to date. To start it, run these two lines in a terminal:

chmod +x Longboard-x86_64.AppImage
./Longboard-x86_64.AppImage
Optional: check that your download arrived intact
DownloadAppImage

Flatpak bundle

For Linux systems that install apps as Flatpaks, such as Fedora Kinoite and Silverblue. Install it with:

flatpak install --user ./Longboard.flatpak

Longboard is for Linux only: there is no Windows, macOS or Android TV version. The downloads are not digitally signed, and the ARM version has not yet been tried on a TV box.

Good to know

Longboard is new, and it is honest about where it stands.

What it can't do today

  • It does not warn you about dangerous websites the way the big browsers do, so take care on sites you don't know.
  • It does not sync between devices or translate pages, and your open tabs are not kept when you close it.
  • It saves a password only when you ask, and only on a system that has a secure password store (a keyring).
  • Ad blocking stops ads from loading on web pages. It cannot remove ads that are part of a video.

What it won't do, on purpose

  • It won't tamper with the copy protection (DRM) on films and shows.
  • It won't pretend to be a TV or another device to get better video quality.
  • It won't skip the ads inside videos or download streaming video.
  • It won't collect or send any information about you.

Longboard is an independent project. It is not affiliated with any website or streaming service, and it does not provide, unlock or bypass access to any content.
